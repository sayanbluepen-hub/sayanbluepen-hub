## 👋 About me [[READ ME IS UNDER DEVELOPED]]

Second-year ECE student at **SRM University AP**, graduating in **2028**.
I like building things that blink, spin, fly, or at least try to. Embedded + hardware is my home base.
Long-term plan: start a deep-tech company around **chip design, space systems and defence tech**.

## 🔧 What I'm working on

- 🚁 **Redundant drone flight controller**: 3 IMUs, 3 MCUs, 3 barometers on the bench, now writing the redundancy logic
- 🧪 **SentiNode**: ESP32 smart lab safety & presence system (PIR, LDR, ultrasonic, gas sensor → NeoPixel, buzzer, servo, LCD)
- 📡 **RF material characterization**: NRW algorithm in MATLAB, extracting permittivity/permeability from VNA S-parameter data
- 📚 **GATE ECE prep**: signals & systems, analog, digital design, circuits. Fundamentals first.
